Frequently Asked Questions

Can I switch plans at any time?
Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time from the Settings page. Changes take effect immediately, and billing is prorated.
What happens when my free trial ends?
After the 14-day trial, you'll be moved to the Starter plan automatically. No charges apply unless you choose to upgrade.
Do you offer refunds?
We offer a full refund within the first 30 days of any paid plan. After that, you can cancel anytime and keep access until the end of the billing period.
What GPU types are available?
Starter plans include NVIDIA T4 GPUs. Professional plans offer A10G GPUs. Enterprise plans include access to H100 GPUs with on-demand scaling.
Is there a limit on API calls?
All plans include unlimited API calls. We only meter compute usage (GPU hours), storage, and outbound network transfer.
Can I add more team members?
Professional plans include 5 seats. Additional seats can be purchased for $12/seat/month. Enterprise plans include unlimited seats.

Ready to get started?

Deploy your first GPU instance in under 2 minutes. No credit card required for the Starter plan.
